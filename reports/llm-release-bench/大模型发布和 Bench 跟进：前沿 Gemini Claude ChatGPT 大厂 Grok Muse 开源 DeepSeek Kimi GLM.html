      2.3 算力到卡：建模与 H100 等效2.3 Compute arrivals: model and H100-equivalents

      这一节把各家芯片的出货量换算成「每月有多少算力真正到了机房」。做法分四步：先取按季的出货颗数（按封装算）；再拆到每个月；再按型号往后挪 1–2 个月，当作到卡并投入使用的时间；最后乘以「每颗折合几颗 H100」，得到 H100 等效。出货数到 2026 年 10 月以 Epoch AI（2026-11-19 版）为主，11 月起来自 SemiAnalysis（2026-11-13）的预测，两家都没有的由本报告推算。只算 Nvidia、Google TPU、AWS Trainium 和 AMD 卖进数据中心的训练和推理卡；华为、寒武纪和各家还没量产的自研芯片不计入。同样是「一颗卡」，算力能差几倍，所以图 2-1 和图 2-2 默认按 8 位算力画，也可以切到按 4 位算力或按颗数。This section converts each vendor's chip shipments into how much compute actually lands in data centers each month. Four steps: take quarterly shipments (counted in packages); split them into months; shift each model forward 1–2 months to when it arrives and enters service; then multiply by H100s per chip to get H100-equivalents. Shipments through October 2026 come mainly from Epoch AI (2026-11-19 release), from November on from SemiAnalysis forecasts (2026-11-13), and anything neither covers is our estimate. Only training and inference chips sold into data centers by Nvidia, Google TPU, AWS Trainium and AMD are counted; Huawei, Cambricon and in-house chips not yet in volume production are excluded. Since "one chip" can differ several-fold in compute, Figs 2-1 and 2-2 default to 8-bit compute and can switch to 4-bit compute or unit counts.

      1. 6–11 月到卡（按 8 位算力）：Nvidia ≈ 1012 万、TPU ≈ 492 万、Trainium＋AMD ≈ 170 万 H100 等效。三家合计 ≈ 1674 万，Nvidia 占 60%。〔中〕
      2. Rubin 在 Nvidia 到卡里从 2026 年 12 月起过半。按 8 位算，9 月 23%、10 月 31%、11 月 45%，12 月 SemiAnalysis 预测的 11 月出货（346,297 颗）到卡，占比到 70%；2027 年上半年在 92%–95%。〔中〕
      3. 按颗数和按算力，排序不一样。2026 年四季度到卡，按颗数前三是 B300（143 万颗）、TPU v7（75.7 万颗）、Rubin（54.0 万颗）；按 8 位算力 Rubin 排第一（477 万），B300 第二（361 万）。2027 年一季度按颗数第二是 Trainium3（66.7 万颗），按 8 位算力第二是 MI450（216 万），Trainium3 不进前五。2027 年二季度按颗数 Nvidia 只比 TPU 多 10%，按 8 位算力是它的 3.2 倍。〔中〕
      4. 按 4 位算，Nvidia 领先更多。2026 年四季度到卡，按 8 位 Nvidia 是 TPU 的 2.2 倍，按 4 位是 3.5 倍：TPU v7 没有 FP4，B300 和 Rubin 的 4 位算力是 8 位的 2–3 倍。〔中〕
      1. Arrivals Jun–Nov (8-bit): Nvidia ≈ 10.1M, TPU ≈ 4.92M, Trainium + AMD ≈ 1.70M H100e. Combined ≈ 16.7M, of which Nvidia is 60%. [medium]
      2. Rubin passes half of Nvidia arrivals from Dec 2026. At 8-bit its share is 23% in September, 31% in October and 45% in November; in December, as the November shipments SemiAnalysis forecasts (346,297 units) arrive, it reaches 70%; in the first half of 2027 it stays at 92%–95%. [medium]
      3. Ranking by units and ranking by compute disagree. For 2026 Q4 arrivals, the top three by units are B300 (1.43M units), TPU v7 (757K units), Rubin (540K units); by 8-bit compute Rubin ranks first (4.77M) and B300 second (3.61M). In 2027 Q1, second place by units is Trainium3 (667K units), while second by 8-bit compute is MI450 (2.16M) and Trainium3 misses the top five. In 2027 Q2 Nvidia has only 10% more units than TPU, but 3.2× its 8-bit compute. [medium]
      4. At 4-bit, Nvidia's lead widens. For 2026 Q4 arrivals Nvidia has 2.2× TPU at 8-bit and 3.5× at 4-bit: TPU v7 has no FP4, while B300 and Rubin deliver 2–3× as much at 4-bit as at 8-bit. [medium]

      到卡时滞Arrival lag

Source: Nvidia's Nov 17 earnings call, where large customers put Rubin into production in about 1 month, half the time Grace Blackwell took [medium]. TPU 1 month (Google's own data centers), Trainium 1 month, AMD 2 months are all assumptions [low]. For comparison, Epoch AI itself assumes a median of about one quarter from sale to operation.

      不计入Excluded
      华为昇腾、寒武纪（中国国内供给，和这三家实验室的算力无关）；Nvidia H20 和 AMD MI308X（中国特供）；Meta MTIA、微软 Maia、OpenAI 自研芯片（量小或还没量产）；SemiAnalysis 表里每季 45 万颗的「Other Blackwell」（推测是 RTX Pro 这类非训练卡）。Huawei Ascend and Cambricon (domestic China supply, unrelated to these three labs' compute); Nvidia H20 and AMD MI308X (China-only parts); Meta MTIA, Microsoft Maia and OpenAI in-house chips (low volume or not yet in volume production); the 450K-a-quarter "Other Blackwell" line in SemiAnalysis's table (likely RTX Pro-class, non-training cards).
      换算系数Conversion factors
      8 位＝稠密 FP8 或 INT8 取大 ÷ H100 的 1,979 TFLOPS，与 Epoch AI 一致。4 位＝稠密 FP4 ÷ 同一个分母，没有 FP4 的卡按 8 位算。Rubin 的 4 位取 NVFP4 训练算力 35 PF；Nvidia 另报的推理 50 PF 没有说明是不是稠密，不用。MI350X 和 MI355X 合并，按 MI355X 的 2.54 算（Epoch 给 MI350X 的是 2.33）。Rubin Ultra 按 Rubin × 2 假设〔低〕。8-bit = the larger of dense FP8 and INT8 ÷ the H100's 1,979 TFLOPS, matching Epoch AI. 4-bit = dense FP4 ÷ the same denominator; chips without FP4 use 8-bit. Rubin's 4-bit uses the 35 PF NVFP4 training figure; the separate 50 PF inference figure is not stated as dense, so it is not used. MI350X and MI355X are combined at the MI355X factor of 2.54 (Epoch gives MI350X 2.33). Rubin Ultra assumes Rubin × 2 [low].
      TPU v8i 为什么不用 5.10Why TPU v8i is not 5.10
      Google 只公布了 v8i 的 FP4 算力 10.1 PF，没有公布 8 位。Epoch AI 把这个 FP4 数当成 8 位，得到 5.10，等于把 v8i 的算力算成两倍。本报告按「8 位通常是 FP4 的一半」推算 8 位 ≈ 5.05 PF，系数 2.55〔低〕；4 位才用 5.10。v8t 同理：FP4 12.6 PF → 8 位系数 3.18、4 位 6.37。哪家做哪颗沿用 SemiAnalysis 和博通 9 月 2 日财报的说法：博通做 8i（Anthropic 2027 年的 5 GW 是 8i），联发科做 8t。代号（Sunfish／Zebrafish）各来源对不上，不影响按 8i／8t 的名字配系数。Google has published only v8i's FP4 compute, 10.1 PF, not its 8-bit figure. Epoch AI treats that FP4 number as 8-bit and gets 5.10, which doubles v8i's compute. We assume 8-bit is typically half of FP4, giving ≈ 5.05 PF at 8-bit and a factor of 2.55 [low]; 5.10 is used only at 4-bit. Same for v8t: 12.6 PF FP4 → 3.18 at 8-bit, 6.37 at 4-bit. Who builds which chip follows SemiAnalysis and Broadcom's Sep 2 earnings: Broadcom builds 8i (Anthropic's 5 GW in 2027 is 8i) and MediaTek builds 8t. The codenames (Sunfish / Zebrafish) are inconsistent across sources, which does not affect assigning factors by the 8i / 8t names.
